What is Computer Network?

A computer network is a system where two or more computers are connected to share data and resources such as files, printers, and internet.

Types of Computer Networks

1. LAN (Local Area Network)

LAN is used in a small area like a home, school, or office.

2. MAN (Metropolitan Area Network)

MAN covers a larger area such as a city.

3. WAN (Wide Area Network)

WAN covers a very large area like a country or the whole world. The Internet is the biggest example of WAN.

Advantages of Computer Networks

  • Easy sharing of data
  • Fast communication
  • Resource sharing (printers, files)
  • Internet access

Disadvantages of Computer Networks

  • Security risks (hacking)
  • High setup cost
  • Virus can spread easily

Conclusion

Computer networks are very important in modern life. They make communication and data sharing faster and easier.
